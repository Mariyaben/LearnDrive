Method & interpretation

Each community is assigned to its nearest facility using straight-line distance in EPSG:32643. A community is served when its distance is at most 1,000 m. All of its population is represented at a single point. Population coverage is served population divided by total population; it is undefined when total population is zero.

Distances use a local projected grid. They do not represent road routes, travel times, opening hours, facility capacity, affordability, or real-world service quality. Choose an appropriate local metre-based CRS before interpreting results. Service circles may overlap and should not be summed as area coverage.
